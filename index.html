💡 Technology Insight

Next-generation voice-to-text solves 5 key problems: (1) Filler word removal for cleaner output, (2) Context-aware punctuation and formatting, (3) Intent understanding beyond literal transcription, (4) Personal dictionary for domain-specific terminology, (5) Multi-language code-switching for global users. The key differentiator is LLM post-processing that transforms raw ASR output into polished, professional text.

💡 Product Insight

Priority Framework: MVP focuses on core transcription quality and cross-platform availability. Growth stage adds enterprise features and compliance. Maturity stage addresses competitive gaps (offline, Chinese, accuracy). Platform stage transforms from product to ecosystem. Key insight: Enterprise compliance and cross-platform coverage provide defensible moats in growth stage, while accuracy improvements (CER < 5%) are critical for maturity stage competitiveness.
